Research dashboard, not investment advice. Each period shows the lowest daily closing price observed within that lookback window, in the listing currency. No percentages. These are closing prices, not intraday lows. A red highlighted Last closing price, with a LOW badge, means it matches the lowest closing price in at least one of the 1-month, 3-month, 6-month or 1-year windows. Hover over red prices to see which periods match. Missing or stale quotes are not highlighted. All quotes are end-of-day and may be delayed or unavailable. Different exchanges use different currencies and calendars. A low return is not proof that a stock is undervalued. Data uses unofficial Yahoo Finance access, which may be rate-limited. To add multiple stocks, click Add / bulk-edit stocks, append rows in the form symbol,isin,exchange,name, then commit changes. The update workflow will fetch their data.
